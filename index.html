About Stars Academy

More Than Education — We Build Futures.

Academic Programs

Our Academic Programs

Five focused tracks, each built around the boards and tests our students are actually preparing for.

Matriculation

Strong foundations for secondary education.

  • Grades 9–10 · Science & Computer Science groups
  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Computer Science, Mathematics
  • Aligned to Punjab & Federal board syllabi

Intermediate

Comprehensive preparation for higher secondary education.

  • FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering & ICS streams
  • Concept-first teaching with regular assessments
  • Direct bridge into our Entry Test track

Entry Test Preparation

Preparation for competitive university entrance examinations.

  • MDCAT, ECAT, NET & other university-specific tests
  • Timed mock tests with detailed score analysis
  • Small-batch problem-solving sessions

O-Level

Internationally recognized academic preparation.

  • Cambridge IGCSE / O-Level syllabus coverage
  • Past-paper driven exam technique
  • Small class sizes for individual attention

A-Level

Advanced academic preparation for university.

  • Cambridge A-Level, AS & A2 streams
  • University & scholarship application guidance
  • Subject specialists for every major group
